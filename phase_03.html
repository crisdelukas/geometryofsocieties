phase_03 — market

Many firms build the same technology and compete for one market. Each firm has humans, who crave, and a model, whose innovation level climbs. Share follows relative innovation, revenue and investors' money feed the climb, and new firms enter while the technology still looks far from what people believe it can become. In one world the humans look only at their own hunger; in the other they also look at the firm just above and the firm just below.

Historical mode: each crab is one lab, entering on the turn of its first scored models and at the level of the best of them, already growing at the craved rate; all labs share the same hunger, and nobody exits (no real lab has). Distance to history is the root-mean-square gap between each crab and its own lab, in innovation units. History panel: Epoch AI, Epoch Capabilities Index (ECI) model scores and Frontier AI Models database, downloaded Oct 2026, CC BY 4.0. Each lab's crab sits at the best ECI it has released so far, minus 90 (ECI's zero point is arbitrary; 90 is the lowest shift at which no lab's first model falls below zero), and jumps when a better model comes out; a lab appears with its first scored model. Market share in the history panel: Similarweb, share of worldwide visits to gen-AI chatbot websites (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, DeepSeek, Grok; everyone else in grey), from its monthly Gen AI traffic updates as published on X and reported by PPC Land, Dec 2024 to May 2026; values between snapshots are interpolated; DeepSeek and Grok were below the 1.5% reporting cutoff in Dec 2024 and are set to 0 there; Grok's April 2026 value is missing and bridged. Consumer web traffic only, not apps, enterprise or API use. Compute dots are single models whose training compute Epoch could estimate; most labs disclose little, so the record is sparse. The window, 30 Nov 2022 to 7 Oct 2026, is stretched over the same number of turns as the models. Both worlds start from the same single pioneer firm and use the same random seed, so differences come from the rules, not from luck. Entries still differ once the worlds diverge, because the chance of entry depends on each world's own frontier. Each firm keeps the colour of its order of entry; the leader's number is in bold; exited firms fade. Concentration is the sum of squared shares (1 = one firm holds everything; 1/N = N equal firms). Money is in arbitrary units.
