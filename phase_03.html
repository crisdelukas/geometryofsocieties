phase_03 — market

Many firms build the same technology and compete for one market. Each firm has humans, who crave, and a model, whose innovation level climbs. Share follows relative innovation, revenue and investors' money feed the climb, and new firms enter while the technology still looks far from what people believe it can become. In one world the humans look only at their own hunger; in the other they also look at the firm just above and the firm just below.

Both worlds start from the same single pioneer firm and use the same random seed, so differences come from the rules, not from luck. Entries still differ once the worlds diverge, because the chance of entry depends on each world's own frontier. Each firm keeps the colour of its order of entry; the leader's number is in bold; exited firms fade. Concentration is the sum of squared shares (1 = one firm holds everything; 1/N = N equal firms). Money is in arbitrary units.
