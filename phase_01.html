phase_01 — rank

Two pairs of crabs climb toward fruits that keep appearing on the horizon. Crab A plays the USSR and crab B the USA; their traits can be tuned so the model resembles the real race, drawn faintly behind each graph. In one pair each crab looks only at the fruits. In the other, each crab also looks at its rival.

All crabs start with a full tank. Climbing one unit of height costs one unit of energy. A crab that runs empty rests until its tank is full again. History panel: each milestone that both sides contested is a fruit, stacked in the order the first one was reached, one fruit spacing apart, so one historical milestone equals one fruit in the models. A fruit appears on the horizon when it was publicly declared as a goal, or otherwise at the first serious attempt. Between milestones each country's climb is drawn as a straight line. 100 turns = Oct 1957 (Sputnik) to Jul 1975 (Apollo–Soyuz), about two months per turn.
